A permanent stage for the park in Tibro, built on a municipal budget of 300,000 kronor. Ordinary timber framing is clad in fibre-cement sheets, hand-painted as faux marble by students at the town's craft academy, so the structure reads as a stack of oversized quarried blocks.

Because the marbling is painted rather than cut, the veining can be bolder and stranger than any stone would allow. It is the first of fifteen speculative proposals from the Tibro Train Tracks study to be built.
