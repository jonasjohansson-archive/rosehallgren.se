Alma Löv Museum of Unexpected Art.

A beginning, made on the meadow across the little stream. A place to call home, built around an interest in ornament. Can ornament add something to a home?

A hands-on process where the sketching happens in direct contact with the material and the surroundings, where exploring is the point and the point is not an ending.
