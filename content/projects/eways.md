Two mall showrooms for an independent electric-vehicle charging operator. The company sells a service rather than a shelf of products, so the shops are built as oversized shop windows, readable in a single glance from the walkway.

Four illuminated pylons stand in a quarter circle facing the mall, one for each kind of customer: private owners, housing co-operatives, offices and public bodies, each with a surface to touch and test. Lit panels are paired with ash; meeting space and back office sit behind.
