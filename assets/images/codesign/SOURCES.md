# Codesign Research Studio — source images

"3, 2, 1, Barneval!" — a countdown campaign by Codesign Research Studio (CoRS),
the non-profit research arm of Codesign, Stockholm, running Sept 2018 to
20 January 2020, the day the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child became
Swedish law.

Images downloaded from https://codesign.se/project/1-2-3-barneval/
(note the transposed digits in their current slug). No photographer is credited
on that page.

Rose Hallgren's credit, verbatim from the original project page under
"Ansvariga inom projektet" (archived 1 Feb 2019 at
https://web.archive.org/web/20190201215434/http://codesign.se/3-2-1-barneval/):

    Jelena Mijanović — Arkitekt / Ansvarig Codesign Research Studio
    Hanna Ivansson — Arkitekt
    Rose Hallgren — Arkitekt
    Daniella Ricci — Arkitekt

She is credited identically on the CoRS "Team 2018/2019" roster and as a
co-author of the ARQ research grant "Utan en femtedel, aldrig hel: Hur
inkluderar vi barn i designprocessen?" (2018).

Note: the current codesign.se was rebuilt and dropped all personnel credits.
They survive only on the Wayback Machine, linked above.

Not used, but available if permission is obtained: Arkitekten's photographs of
the parade and the 2019 kick-off, credited "Foto: Magnus Laupa", at
https://arkitekten.se/nyheter/codesign-havdar-barnens-ratt/

photographs in that post are by Hanna Ivansson and Daniella Ricci Saccotelli and
