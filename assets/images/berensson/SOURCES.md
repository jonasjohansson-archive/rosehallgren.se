# Anders Berensson Architects — source images

These are Anders Berensson Architects projects on which Rose Hallgren is
credited as Architect. Images were downloaded from the studio's public project
pages and resized to 2400px on the long edge; several were trimmed of baked-in
white letterboxing. The credits below are quoted verbatim from those pages.

Every project section on the site carries a visible credit and links back to
the studio's own page.

Notes
- Only Rock Stage names a photographer (Linda Hutchins). The other projects are
  renders and drawings by the office, with no photographer credited.
- The studio's own pages print no year. Years in the site metadata are left as
  [year] placeholders; the WordPress upload paths suggest Rock Stage, Järva
  Stadspark, Marieberg 2022, Stockholm Shorelines 2022, Eways 2021, c/o 2018 —
  inferred, not stated, so confirm before publishing them as fact.
- Her middle name is printed as "Java" on four pages and "Yoriko" on Marieberg.
