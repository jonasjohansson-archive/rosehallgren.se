c/o

2023 · Sweden

Two additions to Sommarnöjen's c/o series of prefabricated summer houses. Buyers usually own remote plots, so c/o 40 arrives as two volumes sized to Swedish road transport limits and is craned together on site, one holding kitchen, bathroom and bedrooms, the other the living room. c/o 50 repeats the arrangement with an extra bedroom.

The work also meant auditing every detail and drawing across the existing range, visiting the Latvian and Estonian factories that build the houses, and partly automating the drawing production so each house generates its own document set.
