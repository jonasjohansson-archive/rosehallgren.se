Fluffy Encounters

2025 · Copenhagen Architecture Biennial

Fluffy Encounters joins the 2025 Copenhagen Architecture Biennial with a combined workshop and exhibition dedicated to the possibilities of wool in architecture. The project explores how this ancient fibre, often discarded today, can support slow, circular, and locally rooted material practices.

Through hands-on making and curated displays of Nordic collaborations, visitors encounter wool at different stages: raw, processed, reworked, and reimagined through innovative products and value-chain efforts.

The workshop invites participants to sort, felt, shape, and engage directly with wool, while the exhibition contextualises these processes within broader cultural, ecological, and architectural conversations.

Together, they highlight how returning to materials and their rhythms can help mend fragmented resource flows and cultivate an architecture grounded in care, collaboration, and slowness.
