Danny Saucedo

2024 · Melodifestivalen, Stockholm

Stage design for Danny Saucedo's "Happy That You Found Me" at Melodifestivalen 2024, built around a physical Romanesque gate and staircase set against three eight-metre LED walls and a nine-metre LED floor.

The gate is the hinge of the whole piece. It is real, built to be projection-mapped, and the projected architecture continues out of it into the screens, so the boundary between what is standing there and what is light is never quite fixed. The visuals move from a moonlit Romanesque evening through a garden like Elysium to a climax where the gate falls back into open sky.

I worked on the scenography and built the physical maquette the team tested the visuals against, which is how the proportions of the gate and its staircase were settled before anything was fabricated at scale.
