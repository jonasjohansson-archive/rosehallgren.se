It's Aalto in the details

Alvar and Aino Aaltos' perspective on the interrelationship between humanity, nature, architecture, and design was unique in their time. How can this perspective be visually interpreted in today's changing environment? How has it evolved, and how can it develop further in the future?

The Paimio Sanatorium and Grafia ry celebrate their 90th anniversaries 2023. To mark the occasion, they held an international poster competition that received almost 500 entries from Finland and around the world. The posters explore the Aaltos' vision and reinterpret it for 2023.

The Human Perspective Poster Exhibition showcases the winning posters along with additional 24 entries chosen by the jury.

Folkbastu Larsberg / Dalénum

Med inspiration från finsk bastukultur och kärlek för form, hantverk och hållbarhet, vill vi föreslå en bastubrygga i området kring Dalénum och Larsberg. En plats för Lidingöborna att få koppla av på och ta del av bastubadandets hälsofrämjande effekter.

Det är något magiskt med bastun, en plats som ger andrum från vardagens plikter. I vårt samhälle, där vi ofta är mer frånvarande än närvarande, finns ett behov av en plats för avkoppling och att vara i nuet.

Ninfeo di Bramate Teatro

An outdoor theatre and park in Genazzano

The main space, open towards the valley, is the loggia and the main stage of the theatre along with the serlianas, formed by three bays separated by large pillars and enclosed between two exedras. Letting this part of the park with its existing structure become a place for creative expression.

A path through the park, through the trees and through the valley in which the ruins rest upon. To the existing historical structure we repeat the circular geometries and enhance them by adding an open roofing in order to shelter the main stage of this outdoor theatre.

Tulip

Vase

Tulips are the only flower that, after being harvested, continues to grow – a beautiful idea to include in the creation of a vase intended just for the tulip.

The vase becomes, in contrast to the flower, a static object that must relate to something living that changes over time. The same richness of variation that we see in the flower.

An interpretation of the iconic Delft pyramid.

Material: steel, clay, tulips

Drip Drop Non Stop

PLX Tjärö 2023

Like the transparent willow leaves that make up the eel in its first stage of life, the viewers, brought together by the currents of life, become aware of their senses. The play between the most basic elements of our surroundings: light, water and sound is embodied by a recurring event, the dripping of water.

A meditative state and a stillness, softly entwined under a water surface. The drops are caught by a vessel which in turn emits a sound. A music of "drip drop" and resonance from the walls of the vessel is staged.

Drömmarens Malmö

Malmö Dreaming

Drömmarens Malmö (Malmö Dreaming) is Fanfaluca Collective's contribution to the architecture festival Malmö in the Making. The project consists of two parts, a film that examines the stories of Malmöiter (people of Malmö) and their connection to their city.

The second complementary element is a built installation where you can take part in the film in the public space. The contribution aims to invite a dreaming about our relationship with the city by conveying different descriptions and perspectives of Malmö.

Within dreaming, everything is possible. Hopes, reflections and relationships float to the surface. Together, we want to create a moment of shared travel in thought, through the spaces of the city.

The Sauna Collective

Larsbergs Torg

Placed on the square of Larsbergs Centrum we find an explosion of colors. A small building with strange spheres appears to have landed on this very grey site one early evening in November, 2020, as if this year couldn't get any weirder. It is hard to tell its origin, let us call the style:

Crazy Bollywood Italian Disco.

Steam comes floating out in the cold autumn air from the pink spheres, and we see figures very much like humans step out in bathing suits to then cool themselves in the water filled tanks in close proximity to the spheres. The function of these structures reminds us of the nordic act and ritual of sauna bathing.

Sonar

A meeting between architecture, form and electronics.

An installation that gave the visitors of Sonár Reykjavik an opportunity to interact with the facade of the Harpa Music Hall, consisting of 714 LED panels. An electronic canvas that was manipulated using different controllers to produce different effects (flash, twist, slide, colourise).

To develop, design and build a physical interface inspired by architecture.
