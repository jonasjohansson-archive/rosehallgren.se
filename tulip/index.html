Tulip

2021 · Blomsterfrämjandet, Klong and Straight Design Studio

A vase made for a single flower. Tulips keep growing after they are cut, so the vessel has to hold something that rearranges itself over the days it stands there. The vase stays still while the flower moves.

The form is an interpretation of the Delft pyramid, the tiered tulip vase of the seventeenth century, when a single bulb could cost more than a house.

Winning entry in the Tulip competition, run by Blomsterfrämjandet with Klong and Straight Design Studio
